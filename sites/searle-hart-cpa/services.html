What we do

The shape of a year

Tax season is one quarter of the work.

Firms that only appear in filing season are reacting to a year that already happened. A year-round relationship means the books stay current, the surprises get found while there is still time to do something about them, and the return is assembled from records rather than from memory.

  1. 1

    Records kept as they happen

    Bookkeeping through the year, not reconstructed after it.

  2. 2

    Reviewed along the way

    Questions raised while the answer can still change the outcome.

  3. 3

    Filing season

    Preparation from clean records instead of a shoebox and a deadline.

  4. 4

    Then straight back into it

    The next year starts the day the last one is filed.
