About the clinic

A local clinic on Stocks Ave.

Premier Therapy Associates is a therapy clinic in Rexburg, Idaho, at 1216 Stocks Ave. Physical, occupational and speech therapy are all provided here, in the same building, by one care team.

The building

Easy to find, easy to park at.

This is the clinic. A single-storey building on Stocks Ave with the name on the front gable and the street number underneath it, and parking right in front of the door.

That matters more than it sounds like it should. Somebody arriving for a first appointment on a walking aid, or with a child in tow, should not have to hunt for the entrance.

How we are set up

What one roof actually changes

Three things, and none of them are marketing. They are the practical differences between one clinic and three.

One place

One address to get to

If your care involves more than one discipline, it is the same drive, the same parking lot and the same front desk each time.

One team

Therapists in the same building

Physical, occupational and speech therapists working in one clinic can talk about a shared patient without an appointment and a fax machine.

One plan

Care that connects

Instead of three separate plans that never quite line up, the pieces are meant to fit together from the start.
