What we offer

Three services, described plainly. Call to talk about which one fits, or whether more than one does.

Physical Therapy

Hands-on treatment to rebuild strength, mobility and movement confidence after an injury, after surgery, or after pain has quietly narrowed what you are willing to do in a day.

Most of the work is progressive: a starting point, a plan, and small steps that add up, with the plan adjusted as things change.

  • Post-surgical
  • Injury recovery
  • Strength and mobility
  • Balance

Occupational Therapy

Practical work on the fine motor skills and everyday tasks that independence actually runs on: dressing, cooking, writing, handling the small things that stop being small when they stop working.

The goal is not an exercise you can do in a clinic. It is the task you need to do at home on a Tuesday.

  • Fine motor skills
  • Daily living tasks
  • Hand function
  • Adaptation

Speech Therapy

Support for communication, swallowing and cognitive-language needs, at whatever age and whatever starting point. Progress is tracked against clear markers so you can see movement rather than take it on faith.

Patience is most of the method here.

  • Speech and language
  • Swallowing
  • Cognitive-language
Why one building matters

Recovery rarely stays in one lane.

A stroke can affect movement, daily tasks and speech at the same time. So can a serious accident. So can a developmental difference in a child. When those three needs are handled by three unconnected clinics, the patient becomes the messenger between them.

Here, physical, occupational and speech therapy sit under one roof at one Rexburg address. If your care needs more than one of them, the overlap in the middle of this diagram is where it happens.
