Repair

Garage door repair

Springs, cables, rollers, tracks, openers, and everything in between. If the door is stuck, noisy, crooked, or off its track, we can diagnose it and tell you what it actually needs.

A broken torsion spring is the most common reason a door quits on a cold Rexburg morning, and it is the one part a homeowner should not try to swap themselves. It holds the entire weight of the door under tension.

  • Springs
  • Cables
  • Rollers
  • Tracks
  • Openers
Installation

New door installation

Replacing a door that has reached the end of its life, or building new? We install garage door systems sized and set up for your opening, residential garages and commercial overhead doors both.

An opening that was framed in the seventies is rarely the size a door comes in today, which is why measuring the opening in person beats ordering off a catalogue.

  • Commercial
  • Replacement
  • New construction
Service

Routine service & safety checks

Tune-ups and safety checks that keep springs, cables, and openers running the way they should, before something breaks on the coldest morning of the year.

A door cycles thousands of times a year. Most of the failures we get called out for were visible months earlier as a frayed cable, a dragging roller, or a door that had gone out of balance.

  • Balance checks
