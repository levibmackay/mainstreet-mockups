About the practice

A familiar name across the Upper Valley.

Toenjes, Brizzee & Orme Dentistry takes care of smiles across two Upper Valley towns: a local family dental practice built around straightforward, friendly visits for every generation.

Call Saint Anthony Call Ashton

One practice, on both ends of the valley.

Saint Anthony and Ashton are small towns, and a family dentist in a small town ends up seeing the same families for a long time: the child who needed the first cleaning, then the teenager, then that person’s own kids.

That is the practice this is. Two offices, one team, and the sort of visit where nobody is being sold anything on the way out.

    Two Upper Valley locations

    An office in Saint Anthony and an office in Ashton: two towns, one family dental practice, each with its own direct phone number.

  2. 2

    An established local practice

    A family-oriented dental practice that is part of the community in Saint Anthony and Ashton, Idaho, rather than a clinic run from somewhere else.

  3. 3

    Every age, one roof

    From the youngest kids to grandparents, the whole family is seen by the same practice, which keeps records and habits in one place.

What a visit is like

Calm, unhurried, and close to home.

The two things families say they want from a dentist are that the appointment does not feel rushed and that they do not have to drive an hour for it. Both offices are built around exactly that.

Not rushed

Unhurried appointments in a relaxed setting. A dental visit that does not feel intimidating is one that people actually come back for.

Close to home

Whether you are in Saint Anthony or up the road in Ashton, the office you need is the one in your own town.
