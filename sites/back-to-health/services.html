What gets looked at

The spine is one system, not three complaints.

A neck that will not turn and a lower back that aches at the end of the day are rarely unrelated. Chiropractic care looks at the column as a whole, because a restriction in one region changes how the ones above and below it move.

That is the reason a visit here starts with questions rather than with a table. Where it hurts and where the problem lives are often two different places.

  • Listening first. The history usually points at the cause faster than the symptom does.
  • Explaining before adjusting. You should know what is being done and why before it happens.
  • Adjusting the plan. Care follows how your body actually responds, not a schedule set on day one.

Hands-on care

Chiropractic Adjustments

The core of what the practice does. Adjustments are hands-on chiropractic care focused on spinal health and on helping your body move without restriction.

Every appointment

Whole-Person Visits

Every visit starts with listening. The point is to understand how your whole body is functioning rather than only the spot that hurts, because the spot that hurts is often downstream of the actual problem.

That takes a few more minutes at the front of an appointment and saves a great deal of time afterwards.

Over time

Ongoing Care

For patients managing pain over a longer stretch, care is built as a plan that fits how your body is actually responding, and it changes when the response changes.

Long-term patients are much of what a practice like this is: people who have been coming to 60 S 2nd W through several chapters of their lives.
