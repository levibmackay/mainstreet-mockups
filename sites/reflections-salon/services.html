Service categories

A short menu, on purpose. Call or text and we will help you land on the right appointment rather than guessing from a price list.

Cut & Style

Precision cuts and finished styling, tailored to your hair and to how you actually wear it day to day rather than how it looks for one hour in the chair.

Bring a picture if you have one. It saves a lot of describing.

  • Cuts
  • Trims
  • Blowouts
  • Finished styling

Colour & Highlights

Full colour, highlights and dimension work. Colour is the service where a conversation up front matters most, because where your hair is starting from decides what is realistic in one appointment.

Talk through the look you want when you call and we will tell you honestly what it will take to get there.

  • Full colour
  • Highlights
  • Dimension
  • Root touch-ups

Special Occasion Styling

Updos and event styling for weddings, dances and the days you are going to look back on in photographs for the rest of your life.

Occasion work books up around dance and wedding season, so call early rather than the week of.

  • Weddings
  • Dances
  • Updos
  • Events

Treatments

Conditioning and restorative treatments to keep hair healthy between visits. Worth asking about if you colour regularly, or if a Rexburg winter has left your hair drier than it was in August.

  • Conditioning
  • Restorative
  • Between visits
Booking

Two ways to get on the books.

You can book online through the salon's booking system, which is open at eleven at night when the salon is not. If you would rather talk to somebody, or if you run into trouble booking online, call or text (208) 356-5856.

For colour especially, a quick conversation before the appointment is worth it. It is the difference between booking an hour and booking the time the work actually needs.
