Six jobs we get called for

If it carries current, it is on this list somewhere. If yours is not, call and describe it.

Panel upgrades

Old or undersized panels pulled and replaced with modern capacity, current breakers, and proper grounding and bonding. Plenty of houses around Rexburg were wired for a 1970s load and are now running an electric range, a shop, a hot tub and two EV chargers off the same service.

If your breakers trip in a pattern, or the panel has a name on it that no electrician wants to see, that is the conversation to have.

  • Service upgrades
  • Breaker replacement
  • Grounding and bonding

Residential wiring

Wired to last, not just wired to pass an inspection and become somebody else's problem later.

How the power gets to you

Where a service call usually ends up.

Almost every residential problem sits somewhere on this line. Knowing roughly where yours is makes the first phone call much faster, so here it is drawn out.

If the whole house is out, the trouble is usually up at the service or the main. If one room is out, it is a branch circuit. If lights dim when a big motor starts, that is a capacity or a connection question and it belongs at the panel end of the line.
