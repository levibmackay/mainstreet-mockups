Worth mentioning on the call

None of it is required. It just gets us to the right answer faster.

First

Whole house, or one room?

That single answer separates a service or main-panel problem from a branch circuit, and it changes what gets brought to the job.

Second

Did anything change?

A new appliance, a remodel, a storm, a well pump replacement. Faults usually start somewhere.

Third

Is it intermittent?

A problem that comes and goes, especially with weather or with a particular motor starting, points somewhere specific.

If it is urgent

Say so up front

Burning smells, hot panels, scorched outlets and sparking should be said at the start of the call, not the end.
