Service index

Five things we get called for.

If what you need is not on this list, call anyway. Most plumbing problems are a version of one of these with a different name attached.

FIG. LK-01

Leak detection & repair

Dripping fixtures, running toilets, and the leaks you can hear but cannot find. We trace it to the source and fix that, not just the symptom.

Where the water appears and where the pipe failed are frequently two different places, and the difference between the two is what decides whether you are paying for a fitting or for drywall.

FIG. WH-02

Water heater service

No hot water, not enough of it, or a unit making noise it should not. We service and replace tank and tankless systems.

Up here a water heater works harder than it does most places: incoming water in February is cold enough that an undersized unit will never quite catch up.

FIG. DR-03

Drain & clog clearing

Slow drains, backed-up lines, and clogs that keep coming back. Cleared properly the first time, with the right tool for the line.

A drain that clogs twice in a season is usually a line problem rather than a clog problem, and it is worth knowing which one you have.

FIG. FX-04

Fixture & pipe replacement

Faucets, sinks, toilets and aging supply lines swapped or repiped cleanly, with materials sized right for the fixture.

The visible half of a fixture swap is the easy half. What is behind the cabinet decides whether you think about it again in three years.

FIG. SV-05

Plumbing repair calls

Something is wrong and it needs a plumber, not a guess. Call and we will talk through what is happening and what it takes to fix it.

Regular hours are Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. There is also 24/7 emergency service for the failures that will not keep until morning.
