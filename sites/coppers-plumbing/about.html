How it started

Years on other people's crews first.

Chandler started Coppers Plumbing after spending years working for plumbing contractors across Eastern Idaho. That is a useful order to do it in: you see how a lot of different outfits handle the same job before you decide how you want to handle it.

What came out of that is a company built on the two things that are easy to say and hard to keep doing. Answer the phone yourself, and do not rush the joint.

  • Local to St Anthony. Based at 338 W 2nd N, in town.
  • Direct communication. Call the number, talk to the plumber. No call-centre script standing between you and an answer.
  • Work done to hold. A soldered copper joint fails if it is rushed. Same standard on every job, small or large.
