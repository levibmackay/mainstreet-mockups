About The Practice

A 1906 house that happens to be a chiropractic office.

Rexburg Chiropractic Center has practiced from a restored home built in 1906, at 105 W. Main St in downtown Rexburg, since 2007. Same corner, same building, nearly two decades.

The Doctors

Dr. Jarem Miller, following his father into the same practice.

Dr. Jarem Miller took the practice over from his father, Dr. Dana Miller. That is a rarer thing than it sounds. It means the office has been handed down rather than sold off, and that a good share of the people walking through the door were adjusted by the last Dr. Miller before they were adjusted by this one.

It also explains the building. A practice that changes hands inside a family tends to stay put, which is why Rexburg Chiropractic Center is still in a 1906 home on Main Street instead of a suite in a business park off the highway.

The Corner

Across from Broulims, on Main.

The office sits on the corner of 1st and Main, directly across from Broulims. If you have shopped in downtown Rexburg you have driven past it, probably without registering that the old house on the corner is a working chiropractic clinic.

That is the whole point of being there. Downtown parking, a two-minute walk from the middle of Rexburg, and a building that nobody confuses with anywhere else in town.
