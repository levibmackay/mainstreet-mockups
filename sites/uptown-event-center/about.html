The Woodman Ballroom, reimagined

A Rexburg landmark, given a second life.

Before it was Uptown Event Center, this room was known as the Woodman Ballroom, a name still spoken by longtime residents of downtown Rexburg.

Call (208) 569-0049 See the room

A room with a past, on the second floor.

The building has been renovated and reopened under a new name, carrying its history forward into a venue built for today’s weddings, receptions and gatherings. The event centre sits on the second floor of Uptown Plaza, at 57 East Main Street.

It is a rare thing to hold your event inside a room with a past. Most venues in a town this size are a hall built for the purpose or a hotel suite that could be anywhere. Uptown gives that history a modern home instead of replacing it.

Who runs it

Locally owned, locally run.

Uptown Event Center is owned and run by Monty and Andrea Spaulding, who previously ran Kettle Embroidery. It is a Rexburg business, on a Rexburg street, answering its own phone.

Bookings are taken directly rather than through an agency, which is why a call gets you a straight answer about your date instead of a form and a wait.
