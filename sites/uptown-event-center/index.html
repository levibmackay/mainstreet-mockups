The Woodman Ballroom, Reimagined

A Rexburg landmark, given a second life.

Before it was Uptown Event Center, this space was known as the Woodman Ballroom, a name still spoken by longtime residents of downtown Rexburg. The building has been renovated and reopened under a new name, carrying its history forward into a venue built for today's weddings, receptions, and gatherings.

It's a rare thing to hold your event inside a room with a past. Uptown Event Center gives that history a modern home.
