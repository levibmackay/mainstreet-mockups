The venue

A room built for the moments worth gathering for.

Uptown Event Center is a renovated downtown venue on Main Street in Rexburg: 3,300 square feet of open, adaptable event space for weddings, receptions and corporate gatherings.

Call (208) 569-0049 See the room
What happens here

Three kinds of evening.

An open room in the heart of downtown Rexburg, set for the events that call for a little more occasion than a hotel conference suite.

Weddings

Ceremonies and celebrations held inside a room with real history, and a bride’s room with a full bath to get ready in on the day.

Receptions

Dinner, dancing and toasts. A kitchen and prep area sits off the main room, so catering has somewhere proper to work.

Corporate events

Banquets, holiday parties and company gatherings, with Wi-Fi, parking, and room for up to 300 guests standing or 250 seated.
