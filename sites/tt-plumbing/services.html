What we do

Scope of work.

General plumbing service for homes and property owners across the Upper Valley, out of Ashton and into St. Anthony, Rigby and Rexburg. Call to talk through your specific job, because every property is different.

Call (208) 652-7373 Contact

N°01

Water heater repair & install

Diagnosis, repair, and replacement of residential water heaters.

A tank that has started running cold halfway through a shower is usually telling you something specific, and it is worth knowing which before you buy a new one.

N°02

Leak detection & repair

Locating and fixing leaks in supply lines, fixtures, and fittings.

Finding the leak is most of the job. The repair is often the quick part once you know exactly where the water is actually coming from.

N°03

Drain & sewer clearing

Clearing slow and blocked drain and sewer lines.

A drain that has slowed down over a season is a different problem from one that stopped overnight, and they do not get the same treatment.

N°04

Repiping & repair

Replacing or repairing aging or damaged plumbing lines.

Older houses in this part of the valley have been through a lot of hard winters, and the pipe that failed is rarely the only one of its age.

N°05

Fixture installation

Installing sinks, faucets, toilets, and other fixtures.

New build, remodel, or a straight swap of something that has given up. Set properly, sealed properly, and left tidy.

N°06

Emergency plumbing

Call for a plumbing problem that cannot wait on a scheduled visit.

Water going somewhere it should not be is the one job that does not get better by waiting until Monday.
