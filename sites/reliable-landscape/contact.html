Before You Call

Four things that make the first call quick.

None of this is required. It just gets to a real answer faster than a back-and-forth does.

  1. 1

    Where the property is

    Town and rough location tells us straight away whether it falls in range.

  2. 2

    Roughly how big

    A city lot and five acres are different jobs, especially for sod versus hydroseed.

  3. 3

    What is there now

    Bare dirt, tired lawn, an existing sprinkler system, or a new build with nothing in the ground yet.

  4. 4

    What is going wrong, if anything

    A dry patch, a zone that will not run, or a valve that will not shut off usually points at the cause before anyone is on site.
