About The Crew

A Rigby crew that does the water and the growing both.

Reliable Landscape and Irrigation works out of Rigby, Idaho, with over sixteen years of experience in the landscape industry behind the work. Sprinklers, sod, hydroseeding, design and maintenance, tree pruning, and water features all sit inside one crew's scope.

Why That Matters

Full-spectrum capability is the rare part.

Most landscape companies specialize in one slice of a property. Somebody does turf. Somebody else does irrigation. A third outfit gets called for the pond. The homeowner ends up coordinating three schedules and owning every gap between them.

Reliable Landscape and Irrigation carries all of it. Because the same crew that draws the sprinkler zones is the crew laying the sod over them and grading toward the water feature, the systems get designed together instead of stitched together after the fact. That is also why the irrigation plan comes first on every job: water decides where everything else can live.
