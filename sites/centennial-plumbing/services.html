Systems first

Plumbing is one system, not a list of parts.

Supply comes in under pressure, waste leaves by gravity, and the water heater sits between them. Almost every call is a problem on one of those three paths, which is why the first thing we do is work out which path it is on.

Trace the problem to its source and the repair is usually smaller than expected. Treat the symptom and you will be calling somebody again next month.

Service detail

Six kinds of work, described plainly.

No packages and no tiers. If what you need is not on this list, ask when you request an estimate.

Drains & sewer

Drain Cleaning

Slow or blocked drains, cleared and inspected so the same clog does not come back next month.

A drain that backs up twice is telling you something about the line rather than about the clog, and that is worth finding out before it becomes a bigger repair.

Hot water

Water Heaters

Repair and replacement for tank and tankless units, sized and installed to code.

Sizing matters more than brand. A unit that is too small for the house will disappoint you every winter no matter what is written on the box.

Find it, then fix it

Leak Repair

Pinpointing the source of a leak, whether it is a supply line, a fitting or a fixture, and repairing it correctly.

Where the water shows up is often not where the leak is. Chasing the stain rather than the source is how a small repair turns into drywall work.

Sinks, taps, toilets

Fixture Install

Faucets, sinks, toilets and other fixtures installed cleanly, with attention to the connections behind the wall.

The visible part of a fixture install takes an hour. The part that decides whether it leaks in three years is behind the cabinet.

Everything else

General Repair

Day-to-day plumbing issues in homes and buildings, diagnosed plainly and fixed without guesswork.

Centennial has been working on Rexburg-area systems since 2007, so the housing stock and the way it was originally plumbed is familiar ground.
