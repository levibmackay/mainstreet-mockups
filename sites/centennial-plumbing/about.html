About Centennial

Locally and family-owned since 2007.

Centennial Plumbing LLC has worked on homes and buildings around Rexburg since 2007. The approach has not changed in that time: diagnose the system, fix the actual problem, leave it working right.

How a job goes

Estimate first, then the work.

Request an estimate and a Centennial representative follows up. Someone comes out and walks the system with you before any price is attached to anything, because a plumbing quote given over the phone is a guess.

Scheduled work happens Monday to Friday, 8:00 am to 5:00 pm. Emergency service runs 24 hours, because a failed supply line does not wait for Monday.

  • Walkthrough first. The estimate follows a look at the actual system.
  • Same-business-day follow-up on estimate requests, per Centennial's own request form.
  • 24-hour emergency service for the failures that are actively causing damage.
