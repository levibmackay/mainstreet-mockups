What We Do

General plumbing work, handled in-house.

These are the categories of work we take on. Every estimate starts with a look at the actual system in your home or building, since the scope and approach depend on what we find.

Drain Cleaning

Slow or blocked drains, cleared and inspected so the same clog doesn't come back next month.

Water Heaters

Repair and replacement for tank and tankless units, sized and installed to code.

Leak Repair

Pinpointing the source of a leak (supply line, fitting, or fixture) and repairing it correctly.

Fixture Install

Faucets, sinks, toilets, and other fixtures installed cleanly, with attention to the connections behind the wall.

General Repair

Day-to-day plumbing issues in homes and buildings, diagnosed plainly and fixed without guesswork.

On-Site Estimates

A walkthrough of the system before any work starts, so you know what you're dealing with.

Why Centennial

What we can actually tell you about ourselves.

No inflated claims here, just the facts that matter when you're choosing a plumber.

  • Established in 2007

    Centennial Plumbing LLC has been operating in Rexburg since 2007, long enough to have seen a wide range of plumbing systems in this area.

  • Local to Rexburg

    We're based here, not dispatched from out of town. That means familiarity with the housing stock and building conditions typical of this part of Idaho.

  • A systems-first approach

    Plumbing is a system, not a collection of parts. We trace a problem back to its source before opening anything up, rather than treating symptoms one at a time.
