Let Us Clip Ya Landscaping was founded in 2009 by Ace and Chase as a way to pay for their undergraduate degrees. It is still a Rexburg landscape installation company, run out of 252 E. 4th N.

The short version

A college side job that turned into a crew.

Two students in Rexburg started clipping yards in 2009 to cover tuition. What the business does now is a long way from that: sprinkler systems, skidsteer and dirt work, paver patios, boulder walls and water features.

The crew brings over thirty combined years of landscape experience to a job. That is thirty across the people on site, not thirty years of company history, and the distinction matters because it is the accumulated hands-on time that shows up in how a wall drains or how a base gets compacted.
