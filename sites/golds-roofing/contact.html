Roughly how old is the roof?

Even a guess narrows things down. A twelve-year-old roof with a leak is a different conversation from a twenty-eight-year-old one.

Helpful

Where is the water showing up?

A ceiling stain, a wet spot in the attic, or ice building along one particular eave each point at different things.
