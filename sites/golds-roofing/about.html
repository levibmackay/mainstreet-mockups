Being small also means the answer you get on the phone is the answer. If a repair will hold, we will tell you it will hold rather than quoting a replacement.

Credentials

What the certifications actually mean

Manufacturer certifications are not badges you buy. They are programs a manufacturer runs, with training and installation standards attached, and they can be withdrawn.

GAF

Master Elite Contractor

GAF's top contractor tier. It carries training requirements and manufacturer-backed warranty options that a non-certified installer cannot offer, and GAF lists us in its own contractor directory.

CertainTeed

Select Shingle Master

CertainTeed's installer designation, which requires ongoing training and holding to their installation standards on every job, not just the ones being inspected.
