What we do

Four things, done properly.

The essentials of good vision care, from a practice that has been doing this in Rexburg since before the interstate reached the valley.

Comprehensive eye exams

Full vision and eye-health evaluations for adults and children. An exam here is a conversation about how your eyes actually behave day to day, not a rush through a refraction on the way to an optical counter.

Prescription eyewear

Frames and lenses fitted on site, so the pair you walk out with was measured on your face rather than mailed to it. The point is a pair you will actually wear every day, not the one that photographed best on a rack.

Contact lens fittings

Fitted and checked in person, with follow-up care if something is not sitting right. A lens that is close enough on paper can still be wrong on the eye, which is the whole reason a fitting is a visit and not an order form.

Ongoing vision health

Vision changes slowly, which is exactly why it is worth having one practice watching it. Rexburg Vision Center keeps being the local point of contact as your eyes change, rather than a one-visit relationship.
