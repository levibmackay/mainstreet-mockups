The long view

Sixty years is not a slogan. It is a record.

Most businesses that open in a college town do not last a decade. Rexburg Vision Center has provided eye care here since 1963, which is long enough to have examined the parents of the patients walking in today, and in plenty of cases their grandparents.

That length of time changes what a practice can offer. A prescription is a snapshot. A file that goes back thirty years is a trend line, and a trend line is what tells an optometrist whether something is drifting or genuinely changing.
