Veteran-owned

A local veteran-owned business.

Rigby Lake Chiropractic describes itself, in its own words, as a local veteran-owned business. That is a short sentence carrying a lot: somebody served, came home to eastern Idaho, and built a practice here rather than somewhere with a bigger market.

What it means for a patient is mostly about consistency. This is not a location of anything. It is one office in one town, and the person you see this year is the person you will see next year.
