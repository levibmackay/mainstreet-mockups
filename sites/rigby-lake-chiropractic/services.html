What we offer

Four kinds of visit, one practice.

Straightforward chiropractic care at every stage of life, in a calm setting off Rigby Lake Drive. If you are looking for something specific, call and ask before you book.

New patient visits

An unhurried first appointment. The point of it is for the practice to understand what brought you in and for you to get your questions answered, before anything else happens. Nobody is diagnosed properly in five minutes.

Family chiropractic visits

Care scheduled around a family's actual week, from younger patients through to grandparents. Being the only chiropractic office a family uses means the practice knows the history rather than reading it off a form each time.

Ongoing wellness visits

Regular appointments for patients who would rather keep on top of things than wait for something to seize up. Frequency is a conversation, not a package sold to you at the first visit.

A local, personal practice

One office in Rigby, focused on the people of this community. No call centre, no rotating list of providers, and the same number answers whether you are booking or asking a question.

Before you come in

What a first visit actually looks like.

Plenty of people have never been to a chiropractor and are not sure what they are walking into. Here is the shape of it.

  1. 1

    Call the office

    (208) 745-5165. Say what is bothering you and roughly when you are free.

  2. 2

    Talk before anything else

    History, what you do all day, what makes it worse, what makes it better.

  3. 3

    An examination

    How you move, where the restriction is, and whether chiropractic is even the right answer for what you have got.

  4. 4

    A plan you agree to

    What care would involve and how often, so you can decide rather than be signed up.
