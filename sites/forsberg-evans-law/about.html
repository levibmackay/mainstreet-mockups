How we work

What to expect from a consultation.

Every matter starts with a conversation. Here is the general shape of that process, offered plainly, with no promises about outcome or timeline, since every matter is different.

I

You reach out

Call the office, or describe your situation briefly in writing. Doing so is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

II

An initial conversation

The office reviews what you have shared and arranges a time to discuss the matter directly, so the details can be understood before any next step is proposed. The office will let you know if it can be of service to you.

III

A clear next step

If representation makes sense, you will be walked through what that would involve, in writing and in plain language, before anything is agreed to.
