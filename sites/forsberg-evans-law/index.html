How We Work

What to expect from a consultation

Every matter starts with a conversation. Here is the general shape of that process, offered plainly, with no promises about outcome or timeline, since every matter is different.

  1. I

    You reach out

    Call the office or submit the contact form below with a brief description of your situation. This is not legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

  2. II

    An initial conversation

    The office reviews what you've shared and arranges a time to discuss your matter directly, so the details can be understood before any next step is proposed.

  3. III

    A clear next step

    If representation makes sense, you'll be walked through what that would involve, in writing and in plain language, before anything is agreed to.
