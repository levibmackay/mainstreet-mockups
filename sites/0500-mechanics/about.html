About the shop

One shop. One street. Since 1990.

0500 Mechanics has run out of 390 S 5th W in Rexburg since 1990. Not a franchise, not a rebrand, not a rented storefront with somebody else's sign still bolted to the wall. The same building, with the same bay doors, for over three decades.

Thirty-plus years on 5th West

A run that long is its own kind of review.

An auto shop does not stay open in one small Idaho town for three decades on marketing. It stays open because the work holds up, the invoice matches the conversation, and the same people keep coming back with the next vehicle.

That is the whole story here, and it is worth more than any slogan: 0500 Mechanics has been fixing Rexburg's cars, trucks and farm rigs since 1990, and it is still doing it from the same address.

How it works
