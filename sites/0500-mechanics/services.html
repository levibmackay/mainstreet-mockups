Service detail

What each job actually involves.

Straight descriptions of the work, without the upsell language. If you are not sure which line your vehicle falls under, call and describe the noise.

DIAG

Diagnostics

Find it before you fix it

A straight answer on what is actually wrong before anything comes apart. Codes are a starting point, not a diagnosis: the point is to trace the fault to its source so you are not paying twice for the same symptom.

ENGINE

Engine & Drivetrain

The mechanical heart

Repairs and maintenance that keep the engine and the driveline running clean, from the parts that wear on schedule to the ones that fail without warning. 0500 Mechanics is a Jasper installer, so a remanufactured engine or transmission is on the table when a rebuild is the right call.

CHASSIS

Brakes & Suspension

Stopping and staying level

The systems that stop you and keep you level, inspected and serviced properly. Idaho winters are hard on both: salted roads eat brake hardware, and frost heave on the county roads outside Rexburg finds every tired bushing you own.

ELEC

Electrical Systems

Batteries, charging, wiring

Batteries, alternators, and wiring gremlins traced down and fixed rather than patched. A no-start at 10 below is rarely just a battery, and finding the real cause is the difference between a fix and a jump start.

MAINT

Routine Maintenance

The small things first

Oil changes, fluids, belts, filters, and the small items that prevent the big ones. Scheduled maintenance is the cheapest repair work there is, and it is the reason a vehicle makes it to two hundred thousand miles in this climate.

GENERAL

General Repair

Bring it in and we will look

If it is mechanical and it is giving you trouble, bring it in. Over three decades of general repair work in one Rexburg shop means very little rolls through the bay door that has not rolled through it before.
