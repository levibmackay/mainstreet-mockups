Residential

Everything from a whole new house to the one outlet that stopped working.

New wiring and new construction

Wiring a house from the ground up, from the service entrance to the last device. Rough in, trim out, and a panel somebody can actually read later.

Remodeling

Additions and remodels get rewired to match what the room does now, not what it did in 1988. Kitchens, bathrooms and basements are the usual three.

Service upgrades

When the panel is full, the service is undersized, or the breakers predate the people living there. Upgrading the service is what makes everything else possible.

Hot tubs

A hot tub needs a dedicated circuit, a disconnect and a real load calculation. It is a small job that goes badly when it is guessed at.

Security lighting

Yard, driveway and building lighting for a home, an office or a farm. Wired in properly rather than plugged into whatever was closest.

Wiring repairs and troubleshooting

The circuit that trips, the lights that flicker, the outlet that runs warm. Finding the fault is most of the job and it is the part experience actually buys you.

  • Heating and AC repair

Agricultural

Farm electrical is its own trade. The runs are long, the loads are motors, and the thing that fails always fails in the middle of a set.

  • Pump controls. Starters, controls and the panel that runs them.
  • Wiring irrigation circles. Power out to the pivot and back.
  • Wiring repairs. Finding the fault before it costs you a day.
  • Maintenance and control design. Before the season, not during it.

The layout of a typical pump control job. The panel is the part worth doing carefully, because everything downstream depends on it.
