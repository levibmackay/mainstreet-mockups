Fifty years in the same valley

The business started in Rexburg in 1974 and it has been an electrical contractor here ever since. Two brothers started it. Their family runs it now.

Very little about the trade has stayed the same across those fifty years. Panels changed, code changed, and what a house is expected to run changed completely. What did not change is that the work either holds up or it does not, and the people who did it are still in town either way.

That is the whole reason a family shop stays a family shop in a town this size.
