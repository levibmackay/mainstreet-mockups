# Image and fact sources — Leishman Electric mockup

Date checked: 2026-08-06

**Do not confuse this business with Legacy Electric** (`sites/legacy-electric/`).
Legacy Electric is a different Rexburg electrical contractor with the same
surname in the family, was emailed 2026-08-03, and has its own fact sheet.
Nothing on this page came from Legacy Electric's site, listings or mockup, and
nothing here may be carried back the other way. Every fact below was sourced
independently against a URL that names **Leishman Electric** specifically.

---

## Images

**Result: zero images.** No photograph appears on any of the four pages and
`sites/leishman-electric/img/` was not created. The page is carried entirely by
CSS and inline SVG (the copper conduit rules, the mark, the schematic diagram
and the service-area sketch are all hand-authored SVG in the page markup).

Both permitted sources were checked:

| Permitted source | Status on 2026-08-06 |
|---|---|
| Their own current website | **There is not one.** `leishmanelectric.com` does not resolve. `dig +short leishmanelectric.com A`, `dig +short www.leishmanelectric.com A` and `dig +short leishmanelectricinc.com A` all returned no A record. There is no live site to take a photo from. |
| Their Google Business Profile | No accessible listing photos. Google/Yelp/YellowPages/BBB profile pages all returned HTTP 403 to a direct request, matching what `leads/call-sheets.md` recorded on the same date. Nothing was read, so nothing was taken. |

**A dead site's archived images were deliberately NOT used.** An Internet
Archive CDX query shows `leishmanelectric.com` had a `/gallery/` page and an
`_imgstore/` directory of images when it was live (last HTML captures 2015,
plus a 2013 capture of an older build). Those images were **not** downloaded
and **not** used. The rule permits the business's *own current website*, and a
domain that stopped resolving is not that. There is also no way to confirm the
archived gallery shots are their own work rather than builder stock, since both
captured builds were hosted templates (hibu / Yell "sm6").

## What could not be verified

- **Whether the business is still actively trading.** Every source used is
  either a directory listing or an archive. No live, business-controlled page
  was loaded today: the domain is gone, and the Facebook page at
  `https://www.facebook.com/p/Leishman-Electric-Inc-100089853185150/` yielded
  only the name and "Rexburg ID" when previously fetched. Confirm on the call
  before treating any of this as current.
- **The phone number ringing this business specifically.** Six sources agree on
  the digits. Nobody has dialled it. Per the pre-send gate in
  `09-verification-protocol.md`, item 6, confirm the number reaches Leishman
  Electric before the mockup link is sent anywhere.
