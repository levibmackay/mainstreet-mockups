What to have ready when you call

None of it is required. It just gets to the right answer faster.

First

Whole building or one room?

That one answer separates a service or panel problem from a single branch circuit, and it changes what gets loaded on the truck.

Second

Did anything change?

A new appliance, a remodel, a storm, a pump swap. Faults usually start somewhere and the timing is a real clue.

Third

Is it steady or intermittent?

A problem that comes and goes, especially with weather or with one motor starting, points somewhere specific.

If it is urgent

Say so first

Burning smells, hot panels, scorched outlets and sparking belong at the start of the call, not the end.
