Worth mentioning on the call

None of it is required. It just gets to the right answer faster.

First

Whole building or one room?

That one answer separates a service or panel problem from a single branch circuit.

Second

Did anything change?

A new appliance, a remodel, a storm, a pump swap. The timing is usually the clue.

Third

Is it intermittent?

Something that comes and goes with weather or with one motor starting points somewhere specific.
