Repair-first plumbing

Six jobs we handle every week in the Upper Valley.

Straightforward diagnosis and repair, explained before any work starts.

Leak Detection & Repair

Hidden slab leaks, dripping supply lines, and slow weeps found and sealed before they become a bigger problem. Whether the leak is under the foundation or behind a wall, the goal is the same: find the source and repair the line, not just chase the symptom.

Drain & Sewer Clearing

Slow or backed-up drains cleared in kitchens, bathrooms, and main lines. We work to address the root cause of a clog rather than just clearing it for the moment, so the same drain isn't backing up again a month later.

Water Heater Repair

No hot water, pilot issues, or a tank that's failing: diagnosed and repaired so you're not stuck with a cold shower. We look at the whole system before deciding what actually needs fixing.

Fixture Repair & Replacement

Faucets, toilets, and valves that run, rattle, or won't shut off, repaired or swapped out for good. Small fixture problems are often the first sign of a bigger issue upstream, so we check for that too.

Whole-Home Pipe Repair

Corroded, cracked, or frozen pipe sections repaired or re-run, in copper, PEX, and everything between. We work with whatever piping is already in the house rather than insisting on a full re-pipe when a section repair will hold.

Emergency Repairs

Burst pipes and active leaks don't wait for business hours to start causing damage. Call and we'll talk through it.
